# Email templates

Copy, fill the brackets, send from your business address. Keep the reference in every subject line so threads stay together.

## Needs more information

**Subject:** Your enquiry [REF]: a couple of questions

> Hi [Name],
>
> Thanks for sending this through. Before I can quote, I need:
>
> - [A photo of the underside with a ruler beside it]
> - [The shaft diameter, measured with callipers if you have them]
>
> Once I have those I'll get a quote back to you within a business day.
>
> [Your name]

## Quote

> Hi [Name],
>
> Here's the quote for your [part].
>
> **What's included**
> - Design: [model the knob from your measurements], [1] fit-test print, [1] round of changes. **$[40]**
> - Printing: [2] × [part] in [PLA, black]. **$[15]**
> - [Postage, tracked: $[12]] / [Pickup in Daisy Hill: free]
>
> **Total: $[67]** [No GST is charged.]
>
> **How it goes from here**
> 1. Reply "I accept" to go ahead.
> 2. I send a PDF drawing (Rev A) showing the dimensions that matter. Check them against your part.
> 3. Reply "Approved Rev A", or tell me what to change.
> 4. Pay the design portion ($[40]) by bank transfer. I print, and it's ready about [2 business days] later.
> 5. Pay the remaining $[27] before posting or at pickup.
>
> **Things to know**
> - [Measurements are from photos, so the fit test is there to catch anything I've got slightly off.]
> - [PLA softens above about 55 °C, so don't leave it in a hot car.]
>
> This quote is valid for 14 days. Terms: [site]/terms
>
> [Your name]
