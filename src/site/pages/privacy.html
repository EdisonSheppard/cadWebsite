What I collect

When you send an enquiry I collect your name, email address, optional phone number and suburb, the description of your job, and any photos, drawings or model files you upload. I also store a one-way scrambled version of your IP address. It's used only to limit spam and can't be turned back into the address.

The site doesn't use advertising trackers or analytics cookies.

How it's used

Your details are used to assess your job, prepare a quote, make the part, contact you about it, and keep records I'm required to keep. I won't add you to a mailing list, sell your details, or share them except as described here.

Your files and designs

Photos and files you upload are stored privately. They aren't publicly accessible and are used only for your job. I won't publish photos of your part or reuse your design without asking you first.

Where it's stored

The website and enquiry records are hosted on Cloudflare. Automated emails are sent through Resend. These providers may store data on servers outside Australia. Payments by bank transfer are handled by the banks. This site never collects card details.

How long it's kept

Enquiries that don't go ahead are deleted within 12 months. Records of completed jobs, including quotes, approved drawings and payments, are kept for five years for tax purposes. Design files may be kept for that time too, so you can reorder. Ask and I'll delete them sooner where the law allows.
