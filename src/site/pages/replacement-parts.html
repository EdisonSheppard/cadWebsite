What to send

  • Photos from the front, side and underneath, with a ruler or tape measure in shot.
  • Measurements of anything that has to fit: shaft diameters, hole spacing, overall size. Callipers are best, a ruler is fine to start.
  • The broken part, if you can drop it off or post it. Having the original makes the fit far more reliable.
  • Where it goes: indoors or outside, in the sun, near heat, or taking a load.

What you get

  • A quote that says what's included and how many fit tests.
  • A PDF drawing showing the fit-critical dimensions, for you to check before printing.
  • The printed part, checked against the drawing or your original.
  • The STL and STEP files on request, so you can reprint it later.

About fit

Parts copied from photos alone can be slightly off. When the fit really matters, a quick test print comes first, or you lend me the original or the part it mates with.

A printed copy won't be the same plastic as the factory part. If the original was a stressed hinge or a clip that flexes thousands of times, I'll tell you whether a print will hold up.

Typical jobs

  • Furniture feet, glides and end caps
  • Battery covers, vent covers and blanking plates

  • Light-duty brackets and spacers
