Quotes

Quotes are free and valid for 14 days. A quote covers the scope, quantities, material, revision rounds and fit tests it lists, and nothing else. If what you need changes, I'll send a new quote before doing the extra work. A quote is accepted when you reply to say so.

Drawing approval

For jobs that include design work, I send a PDF drawing marked with a revision letter. The highlighted dimensions are the ones that matter for fit. Please check them against your part. When you approve a revision, I make exactly that revision. Changes after approval create a new revision that you approve again. They may cost extra if they're beyond the revision rounds in your quote.

If a part was made to an approved drawing but doesn't fit because a measurement you supplied was wrong, fixing it is a new revision. If it doesn't match the approved drawing, I'll fix or remake it at no charge.

Fit and suitability

Printed parts are made from FDM plastic. They show layer lines, aren't identical to injection-moulded originals, and are weaker in some directions. Parts made from photos alone may need a fit test. I'll tell you if I don't think a print suits your use, but you're responsible for how the part is used once it's installed.

I don't make parts that are safety-critical, medical, food-contact or for high-temperature use. I don't make weapons, weapon parts, or anything illegal to own in Queensland.

Payment

By bank transfer, or cash at pickup. Design work is paid once you approve the drawing and before printing. Printed parts are paid before posting or at pickup. Larger jobs may need a deposit, as stated in the quote. Files from CAD-only jobs are sent once payment is received.

Cancelling

You can cancel at any time. If you cancel before work starts, there's nothing to pay. If you cancel after design or printing has started, you pay for the work completed, which won't be more than the quoted price. Any deposit above that is refunded.

You must have the right to have any file, design or logo you send me copied or printed. I may decline jobs that look like they infringe someone else's design, trade mark or copyright.

Once you've paid for design work, you may use the files I deliver however you like. I keep a copy so you can reorder, and I won't sell or publish your design without asking. I may reuse general techniques, but not your specific design.
