Custom mounts and enclosures

A bracket for the thing nobody makes a bracket for. A case with the holes in the right places. Designed around your equipment and checked with you before it's printed.

Quote a mount or enclosure

What to send

  • The equipment: make and model, or measurements and photos if it's custom.
  • Where it mounts: wall, desk edge, rail, pole or inside another case, and the space available.
  • Hardware: screw sizes, hole patterns, or that you want me to choose.
  • For enclosures: board type, connector positions, cable entry, buttons, displays and how much airflow it needs.

What you get

  • A PDF drawing showing mounting holes, clearances and openings to check.
  • A fit-test print for anything with tight clearances, if the quote includes it.
  • The final part, with inserts, screws or magnets fitted when they're listed in the quote.
  • STEP and STL files on request.

Limits

Printed mounts suit light, indoor loads. I won't make mounts for anything overhead that could hurt someone if it fell, or enclosures for mains-voltage equipment.

Cases for boards like the Raspberry Pi and ESP32 are a speciality. I build with them myself.

Typical jobs

  • Raspberry Pi, ESP32 and Arduino cases with cut-outs for your exact wiring
  • Sensor housings and weather-sheltered mounts
  • Desk, monitor and under-shelf holders
  • Adapters between mismatched hose, pipe or tool fittings
  • Organisers and drawer inserts sized to what you're storing
