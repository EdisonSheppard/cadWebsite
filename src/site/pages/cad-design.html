New models

Send a dimensioned sketch, photos with measurements, or a description of what it has to do and fit. I model it in Fusion and send a PDF drawing of the key dimensions for you to check.

Modifications

Move or resize holes, add cut-outs, change thicknesses, or adapt a model to fit something else. Editable STEP or Fusion files are quick to change.

An STL is a mesh, not an editable model. Changing one properly usually means remodelling the part, so I'll say in the quote if that's the case.

What you get

  • STEP for editing and manufacturing.
  • STL or 3MF ready to print.
  • A PDF drawing of the critical dimensions.
  • Native Fusion files if agreed in the quote.

You approve the final revision and the files are sent to you. There's no print job unless you add one.
