How it works

Every job is quoted by hand, and nothing is printed until you've approved exactly what will be made.

  1. You send an enquiry

    Use the quote form. Describe what the part does and what it has to fit, and add photos, measurements or files. You get a reference number straight away.

  2. I check it's a sensible job

    I look at size, material, where it'll be used and whether a print will hold up. If something's missing, I'll ask. If a printed part is the wrong answer, I'll say so.

  3. You get a quote

    The quote lists what's included, the material and colour, the number of fit tests and revision rounds, the price and an estimated ready date. It's valid for 14 days. Reply to accept it.

  4. You check the drawing

    For any job with design work, I send a PDF drawing with a revision letter (Rev A, Rev B and so on). It shows the dimensions that matter for fit, which are highlighted. Check them against your part, then reply with changes or "Approved Rev B". Approval applies to that revision only. If anything changes afterwards, you'll get a new revision to approve.

  5. Printed and checked

    The approved revision is printed and checked against the drawing, or against your original if I have it. If a fit test was quoted, you try it before the final print.

  6. Pickup, postage or files

    Pick up in the Daisy Hill area by arrangement, or have it posted with tracking. CAD-only jobs are sent as files once you approve the final revision.

Payment

By bank transfer, or cash at pickup. For jobs with design work, the design part is paid once you approve the drawing and before printing starts. Printing is paid before posting or at pickup. Larger jobs may need a deposit, which the quote will state.

Turnaround

I aim to reply to enquiries within one business day. A small print from a ready file is usually ready one to two days after you accept. Simple design and print jobs usually take two to four business days once the drawing is approved.

Each quote gives a ready date. Postage time is extra.

Revisions

The quote says how many revision rounds are included, usually one or two. Changes beyond that, or changes to what the part needs to do, are quoted before I start on them.

If a part doesn't fit because of my mistake, I fix it at no charge.
