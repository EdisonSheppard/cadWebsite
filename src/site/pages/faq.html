Frequently asked questions

Do I need a 3D model?

No. For replacement parts, mounts and new designs, photos and measurements are enough to start. Files are only needed if you want something printed as-is.

How do I measure the part?

Digital callipers are best, and they're cheap at hardware stores. Otherwise use a steel ruler and take photos straight-on with the ruler beside the part. Measure anything that has to fit something else: shaft diameter, hole spacing, overall length and thickness. If you can lend me the original, that's better than any photo.

Will it fit first time?

From an original part or good measurements, usually yes. From photos alone, not always, which is why quotes for tight-fitting parts can include a cheap fit-test print first. The drawing you approve lists the dimensions I'm working to, so we agree on them before printing.

What files do you accept?

For printing: STL, 3MF, STEP and OBJ. For reference: photos (JPG, PNG, HEIC), PDFs and sketches. Fusion archives (F3D, F3Z) are welcome too. Up to {{ site.upload.maxFiles }} files and {{ site.upload.maxTotalMb }} MB per enquiry. If yours are bigger, send what you can and I'll arrange the rest.

How big can a part be?

Up to {{ site.printer.buildMm }} mm in each direction in one piece. Bigger parts can sometimes be printed in sections and joined, but the joint is a weak point, so I'll tell you whether that suits your part.

By bank transfer, or cash at pickup. The how it works page explains when each part is due.

What happens to my files and photos?

They're stored privately and used only for your job. I don't publish or reuse your designs or photos without asking. See the privacy policy.

Do you do 3D scanning, resin or metal printing?

No. Reverse engineering is done from the physical part, measurements and photos, and printing is FDM plastic only.
