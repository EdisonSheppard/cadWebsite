Nothing gets printed until you've checked it

  1. Send what you have

    Photos with a ruler in shot, rough measurements, the broken part, or a file. You don't need a 3D model.

  2. Get a fixed quote

    I tell you what's included, the material, how many fit tests and when it'll be ready. Nothing is charged until you accept.

  3. Check the drawing

    For design jobs you get a PDF drawing with the dimensions that matter. Ask for changes or approve that exact revision.

  4. Printed and checked

    The approved revision is printed and checked against the drawing, or against your sample if I have it.

  5. Pickup or post

    Collect in the Daisy Hill area or have it posted. CAD-only jobs are delivered as files.

More about the process, payment and turnaround

Good fits

  • Appliance and furniture knobs, caps and feet
  • Clips, covers, spacers and simple guides
  • Light-duty brackets and wall mounts
  • Electronics cases and sensor housings
  • Adapters between two things that don't quite fit
  • Prototypes and fit-test versions of your own design

Not a fit

  • Anything safety-critical: brakes, child seats, climbing or lifting gear
  • Medical devices or parts that touch food
  • Parts that run hot, like engine bays or near heaters
  • Resin, metal or CNC-machined parts
  • 3D scanning or custom circuit boards

Not sure? Ask anyway. I'll tell you honestly if a printed part is the wrong answer.
