HOW IT WORKS

From a problem
to a finished part.

01 /

Tell me what you need

Send dimensions, photos, a sketch or a CAD file. Explain how it needs to fit and where it will be used.

02 /

Get a clear quote

I review suitability, then outline the scope, price, included revisions and a proposed ready date.

03 /

Review the design

Where CAD is involved, inspect the proposed model and confirm the important details before manufacture.

04 /

Make, check, hand over

After approval and agreed payment, the part is made, inspected and arranged for collection or delivery.

ABOUT THE WORKSHOP

Built with care.
Made to be useful.

I focus on practical, one-off parts and small projects that benefit from a measured, hands-on approach. I work directly with customers on the design, fabrication and fit details.

Based in Logan, Queensland, with collection and local arrangements by agreement. Materials, application suitability, lead time and scope are assessed before a job is accepted.

No safety-critical parts, unverified high-temperature parts, custom PCB fabrication, or professional 3D scanning offered.
